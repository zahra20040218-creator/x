الخريطة — MapPickerView
Real Baghdad geometry — Karrada at 33.2988, 44.4137, rendered from OpenStreetMap data and used here as a still. Chrome, pin behaviour and every message come from map_picker_view.dart, map_state.dart and strings.dart. In the app the pin is fixed and the map moves — the whole screen is the target, so a thumb never has to hit a small marker. Map imagery © OpenStreetMap contributors.
